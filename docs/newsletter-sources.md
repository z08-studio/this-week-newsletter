## Editorial policy

Use the publication's stated usual weekday in its own publishing context, without converting it to a delivery time in another country. Prefer an explicit publisher statement. Otherwise infer the usual weekday from dated official issues and label that basis `recent-archive`. Holiday gaps, one-off newsflashes, and delayed editions are not new recurring schedules. Store the checked date and exact evidence in each source record.

The free edition is the default when paid and free editions have different days. Daily digests, paused newsletters, irregular publications, and newsletters without usable schedule evidence were not added. Existing studio titles keep the editor-confirmed weekdays. This is a recurring reading directory, not a feed of issues scheduled to publish on a particular date.

## Not included in this batch

- Pointer currently describes itself as twice weekly, so it was not treated as a once-weekly digest.
- The unrelated beehiiv publication named The Changelog announced a pause in August 2026. It is not the Changelog News publication listed here.
- CSS Weekly's recent accessible archive showed varying weekdays and did not provide a stable current schedule for this batch.
- The Batch could not be verified through its official site during this research run. It was not assigned a weekday from memory.

Revisit weak or exception-prone schedules before the next directory expansion. Review new Google Form suggestions manually; never import submissions directly into the live directory.
